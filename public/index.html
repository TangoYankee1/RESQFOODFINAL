How ResQFood Works

Connecting donors, volunteers, and verified organizations to support the Lahug community.

  1. 01 · Donate

    Share surplus food

    Post available food, its quantity, and pickup schedule to help organizations serving Barangay Lahug.

  2. 02 · Deliver

    Volunteers connect the community

    Volunteers collect donations and deliver them to verified organizations, with status updates along the way.

  3. 03 · Receive

    Request through verified organizations

    Residents select their sitio and submit a request. Organizations confirm availability and collection details.
